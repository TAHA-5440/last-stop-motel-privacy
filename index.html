Last Stop Motel: No Refunds! — Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · Android app com.metcosoln.laststopmotel.game

The Android game

This policy covers the Android edition of Last Stop Motel: No Refunds! The game works offline, does not require an account, and has no advertising, analytics, purchases or developer-operated game server.

Information on your device

The game stores your motel progress, tutorial progress, audio preferences and graphics settings in the app’s local storage. These records are used to save and restore your game. The game does not send them to the developer.

Collection and sharing

The Android game does not collect or share personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or gameplay analytics. Its Android runtime and haptic feedback do not add an advertising or analytics service. The CrazyGames integration used by the separate web edition is disabled in the Android build.

Retention and deletion

Local game records remain on your device until replaced, cleared through Android’s app-storage settings or removed when the app is uninstalled. Android backup and restore may retain or restore app data according to your device and Google account settings. The developer does not receive that backup. There is no game account to delete.

Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request, do not sell it or use it for advertising, and retain it only while needed for support. Email is handled by the email providers involved. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by contacting us.

Children’s information

The Android game does not request or knowingly collect personal information from children. Please do not send a child’s personal information in a support request. Contact us if you believe such information has been sent so it can be removed.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the game’s data practices change. For privacy questions or requests, contact metconsoln@gmail.com and mention Last Stop Motel.
